## AI Regulations
### EU AI Act
- **Description**: The EU AI Act is a landmark regulation that categorizes AI systems based on risk levels and imposes compliance requirements.
- **Impact on Automotive Industry**:
  - High-risk AI systems in vehicles (e.g., autonomous driving) must comply with strict transparency, data governance, and risk management requirements.
  - Manufacturers like Renault must ensure AI systems are auditable and meet EU safety standards.
- **Compliance Deadline**: 2024-2026 (phased implementation)
- **Source**: [EU AI Act (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32023R1689)

## Sustainability Regulations
### EU Green Deal and Circular Economy Action Plan
- **Description**: The EU Green Deal aims to make Europe the first climate-neutral continent by 2050, with a focus on sustainable production and consumption.
- **Impact on Automotive Industry**:
  - Renault must adhere to circular economy principles, including recycling materials and reducing waste in vehicle production.
  - Compliance with sustainability reporting and carbon footprint reduction targets.
- **Compliance Deadline**: Ongoing, with milestones in 2030 and 2050
- **Source**: [EU Green Deal (European Commission)](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/IP_23_3683)

## Emissions Regulations
### EU Emissions Standards (Euro 7)
- **Description**: The Euro 7 standard sets strict limits on pollutants such as NOx, CO, and particulate matter from vehicles.
- **Impact on Automotive Industry**:
  - Renault must ensure all new vehicles comply with Euro 7 emissions limits.
  - Non-compliance may result in fines and restrictions on vehicle sales in the EU.
- **Compliance Deadline**: 2025
- **Source**: [EU Emissions Standards (EUR-Lex)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32019R0631)

## Conclusion
Renault must comply with a range of EU regulations related to AI, sustainability, and emissions. Key actions include:

- Complying with Euro 7 emissions standards to avoid penalties.
